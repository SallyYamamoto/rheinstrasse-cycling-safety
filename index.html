About the observed traffic data

Recording: Filmed on 12 October 2022 along Rheinstrasse, Munich. TUMDOT-MUC used 12 drones at six locations to cover approximately 700 m during the afternoon.

From video to open data: Road users were detected, classified and tracked in drone footage. Processing removed misdetections and stationary objects, joined tracks interrupted by occlusion, interpolated gaps and aligned the videos in space and time. The published CSV records positions every 0.08 seconds.

In this viewer: Only part 2 of the published CSV is used (10 minutes). Passenger car and bicycle trajectories were extracted, converted from UTM zone 32N to geographic coordinates, aligned vertically with the CityGML model and displayed using 3D assets. Vehicles in the intersection safety scenarios are simulated; they are not part of this observed traffic flow.

Parked vehicles: Static placements from 230 vehicle footprints created by the project author for the visibility analysis using DLR inputs. They are excluded from the moving car/bicycle proximity test.
